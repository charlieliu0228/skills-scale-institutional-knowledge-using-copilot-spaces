# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises. These personas describe areas of accountability, not required staffing: depending on project size and context, one person may fill multiple roles.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX Designers / User Researchers

### Role Summary
UX Designers and User Researchers understand user needs and turn them into usable, accessible experiences. They may conduct research, design workflows and interfaces, and validate solutions with users.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and conduct user research, and share findings with the team
- Create user journeys, prototypes, and design guidance
- Validate usability and accessibility with users and stakeholders
- Identify design assumptions, research needs, and experience risks

### Goals
- Ensure solutions address real user needs
- Make experiences clear, usable, and accessible
- Reduce the risk of building features users cannot successfully use

### Typical Communication
- Research findings, user journeys, and design reviews
- Usability test plans and results
- Design handoffs and discussions of open questions or dependencies

### How They Work with Existing Roles
- Partner with Product Managers to understand user needs, shape priorities, and validate product decisions.
- Work with Developers to explain design intent, assess implementation options, and validate the implemented experience.
- Coordinate research and design milestones with Project Managers, surfacing dependencies, constraints, and risks.

## QA / Test Engineers

### Role Summary
QA / Test Engineers help the team prevent defects and evaluate whether a solution meets its quality expectations and acceptance criteria.

### Responsibilities
- Define a test approach based on project risks and requirements
- Prepare and execute appropriate tests, including exploratory and automated testing where useful
- Record, communicate, and help triage defects
- Report quality risks and evidence relevant to release readiness

### Goals
- Find important issues early and support reliable releases
- Provide clear evidence of coverage, defects, and remaining risk
- Make quality expectations part of delivery rather than a final-stage check

### Typical Communication
- Test plans, test results, and defect reports
- Quality risks and coverage updates during planning and delivery
- Release-readiness input shared with the delivery team

### How They Work with Existing Roles
- Work with Developers to clarify expected behavior, improve testability, and reproduce and resolve defects.
- Align with Product Managers on acceptance criteria and the severity of user-facing issues.
- Coordinate test timing and dependencies with Project Managers, escalating schedule or release risks early.

## DevOps / Release Engineers

### Role Summary
DevOps / Release Engineers support the systems and practices that build, deploy, and operate software reliably.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain or improve build, test, and deployment pipelines
- Coordinate environment and infrastructure readiness
- Plan deployment, monitoring, and rollback approaches with the team
- Identify operational risks and communicate incidents or readiness concerns

### Goals
- Make delivery repeatable and dependable
- Support safe releases and effective monitoring
- Reduce avoidable deployment and operational disruption

### Typical Communication
- Pipeline, environment, and deployment updates
- Release plans, operational checklists, and monitoring information
- Escalations for infrastructure or production risks

### How They Work with Existing Roles
- Work with Developers on build pipelines, environments, deployment needs, and operational feedback.
- Coordinate release schedules, dependencies, and readiness checkpoints with Project Managers.
- Keep Product Managers informed about launch constraints and operational readiness that may affect delivery or users.

## Security / Privacy Specialists

### Role Summary
Security / Privacy Specialists advise teams on protecting systems and data, meeting applicable requirements, and managing security and privacy risks.

### Responsibilities
- Identify security and privacy requirements, threats, and risks
- Recommend appropriate controls and review designs or implementations
- Advise on data handling, access, retention, and incident considerations
- Escalate material concerns and communicate required follow-up

### Goals
- Reduce security and privacy risks throughout delivery
- Make relevant obligations and controls clear to the team
- Enable informed decisions and timely remediation

### Typical Communication
- Risk assessments and actionable review findings
- Security and privacy requirements or design guidance
- Escalations and follow-up on mitigations

### How They Work with Existing Roles
- Partner with Developers to incorporate controls into design, implementation, and testing.
- Work with Product Managers to clarify requirements and assess trade-offs that affect users or data.
- Help Project Managers track reviews, mitigations, dependencies, and escalations in project plans and risk records.

---

## Business Sponsors / Stakeholder Representatives

### Role Summary
Business Sponsors and Stakeholder Representatives connect project work to organizational goals and provide direction or decisions within their authority.

### Responsibilities
- Communicate business objectives, constraints, and stakeholder needs
- Make or obtain timely decisions and approvals within their authority
- Review progress and raise changes in business context or priorities
- Help resolve escalations that require stakeholder alignment

### Goals
- Ensure project outcomes support business objectives
- Enable timely decisions and clear accountability
- Maintain alignment between the delivery team and affected stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Outcome and priority discussions with Product Managers
- Status, decision, and escalation updates with Project Managers
- Reviews at agreed milestones and decision points

### How They Work with Existing Roles
- Align with Product Managers on desired outcomes, priorities, and the business context for product decisions.
- Work with Project Managers on status, dependencies, risks, decisions, and escalations, providing direction or approvals when needed.
- Provide Developers with business context through the Product Manager or agreed project channels, avoiding conflicting or unprioritized requests.
