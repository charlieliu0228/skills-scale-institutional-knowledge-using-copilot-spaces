# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This folder brings together OctoAcme's project management processes, roles, and working practices. Projects move through initiation, planning, execution, release, and retrospective: teams validate the need and desired outcomes before approval, turn approved work into prioritized and estimated increments, track delivery against milestones, and capture lessons to guide continuous improvement.

Project Managers coordinate delivery, schedules, risks, and communications; Product Managers define outcomes and priorities; developers build and test; QA validates quality and acceptance; and stakeholders provide input and approvals. Teams use project boards, regular standups and delivery syncs, demos, stakeholder updates, and blocker escalation to maintain shared visibility. Risk and dependency management continue throughout the lifecycle.

Quality is integrated into development and release through unit and applicable integration tests, end-to-end smoke tests for critical flows, CI tests and linting, security scanning, and manual QA when needed. Releases require acceptance criteria and checks to pass, release notes, smoke tests, stakeholder communications, and a rollback or mitigation plan. Retrospectives after sprints, releases, milestones, or incidents turn learning into owned improvement actions.
